# APL (American Premier League) quick reference: match length and substitutions

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16 from apl-rules.md in this folder (a Spring 2022 edition; dates and fees in it
> may be out of date). If this sheet and the full document ever disagree, the full document wins.

FIFA/USSF Laws of the Game apply unless stated otherwise (Article 7 lists the deviations).

## Match length (Article 1, Article 7 Law 7)
- Two 45-minute halves plus stoppage time; halftime no more than 10 minutes.
- Playoffs: if tied at the end of normal playing time, kicks from the mark (no extra time).

## Substitutions (Article 7 Law 3)
- Unlimited substitutions at certain stoppages, with the referee's approval:
  - BOTH teams: on a goal kick, at a kick-off (after a goal or between periods), during an extended time out
    (e.g. an injury), or when the referee stops play to issue a caution or send-off.
  - Team IN POSSESSION only: on a throw-in or corner kick. The opposing team may also substitute if the team in
    possession chooses to substitute.
- Max 18 players may participate in a match; max 7 substitutes on the bench (wearing pinnies).

## Discipline (Article 5)
- 4th yellow card in a season = 1-match suspension. Two yellows in one match = send-off, no automatic next-match
  suspension (the yellows still count toward the tally).
- Straight red = minimum 1-match suspension (3 matches for unsportsmanlike conduct).
