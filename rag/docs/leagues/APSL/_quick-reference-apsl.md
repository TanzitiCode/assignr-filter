# APSL (American Premier Soccer League) quick reference: match length and substitutions

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16 from the official document(s) in this folder. If this sheet and the full document ever disagree, the full document wins.

Source: APSL Regulations & Club Manual 2026-2027.

## Match length
- No league modification: IFAB Laws of the Game apply, 2 x 45-minute halves.
- Playoffs: if tied at full time, two 15-minute extra-time periods (no golden goal), then kicks from the mark.

## Substitutions
- FIFA/IFAB Laws for high-level amateur competition: each team may use SEVEN (7) normal substitutes, NO re-entry.
- Plus a maximum of one concussion substitute per match after an assessment by a trained concussion evaluator,
  regardless of substitutes already used (may be a previously substituted player if all normal subs are used).
  When a concussion substitute is used, the opponent may use one additional substitute for any reason.
- Substitutes present an APSL substitution card to the 4th official or referee (a different color for concussion subs),
  and must wear distinctive bibs/vests.
- Up to 20 players may dress per match.
