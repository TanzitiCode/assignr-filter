# CSOA (Commonwealth Soccer Officials Association) quick reference: Fall 2026 school games

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16 from the official document(s) in this folder. If this sheet and the full document ever disagree, the full document wins.

Sources: CSOA 2026 Fall Private Schools Quick Rules Reference; CSOA 2026 Fall Public Schools Quick Rules Reference.

## Private schools (Fall 2026)
- Varsity halves: 40 minutes (for both IFAB and NFHS games)
- JV halves: 35 minutes for WCAC, VCAC, ODACS (and when not otherwise indicated); 30 minutes for MAC, IAC, ISL, Independents
- Middle school halves: 30 minutes unless otherwise indicated. GPAC middle school only: 30 minutes (A group), 25 minutes (B group)
- Overtime: none in the regular season, EXCEPT
  - MAC: 2 x 5 minutes, golden goal (must be played if inter-conference)
  - IAC: 2 x 5 minutes, golden goal
  - ISL: 2 x 5 minutes, full time
  - Check with coaches before the game; OT may be waived if both coaches wish.
- IFAB games, substitutions: on goals, goal kicks, and possession throw-ins (or piggyback if checked in).
- IFAB mercy rule (GPAC only): with a goal differential above 3, periods are shortened to 20 minutes.

## Public schools (Fall 2026): NFHS rules apply
- Arlington middle schools: one referee, 25-minute halves, no overtime, typical start 3:30 pm, intentional heading
  not allowed (indirect free kick).
- Prince William middle schools: boys play in fall, girls in spring; one referee, 30-minute halves, overtime 2 x 5 minutes
  FULL time, typical start 5:15 pm; mercy rule: game ends with a 10+ goal lead after 3/4 of the game; intentional
  heading not allowed (indirect free kick).
