# DCPL (DC Premier League) quick reference: match length and substitutions

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16 from the official document(s) in this folder. If this sheet and the full document ever disagree, the full document wins.

Source: DC Premier League rules (dcpremierleague.com/league-rules), retrieved 2026-10-16. Games are played per the FIFA
Laws of the Game except as modified here.

## Match length
- Two 45-minute halves, 5-minute halftime.
- Stoppage time is used, but no more than 5 minutes per half.

## Substitutions
- Unlimited substitutions, with the referee's consent, at:
  - a throw-in (either team)
  - a goal kick (either team)
  - after a goal (either team)
  - an injury stoppage: the injured player's team may substitute; the opponent may then substitute ONE player
  - a yellow card: the referee may call for immediate substitution of the cautioned player
- Players need the referee's consent to enter or leave; the referee may decline a request.

## Other
- Forfeit if a team doesn't have 7 players ready within 10 minutes of kickoff, or doesn't provide a roster/photo IDs.
- Slide tackles are permitted. 5 yellow cards in a season = 1-game suspension.
