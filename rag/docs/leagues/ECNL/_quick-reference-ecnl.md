# ECNL quick reference: game length and substitutions (2026-27)

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16 from the official document(s) in this folder. If this sheet and the full document ever disagree, the full document wins.

Source: 2026-2027 ECNL Competition Rules (ecnl-competition-rules-2026-27.pdf), Rules 3.9 and 3.18.

## Game length (Rule 3.18), ECNL halves by age group
- U11: 2 x 30 minutes
- U12: 2 x 30 minutes (2 x 35 minutes when playing 11v11)
- U13: 2 x 35 minutes
- U14: 2 x 40 minutes
- U15: 2 x 40 minutes
- U16: 2 x 40 minutes
- U17: 2 x 45 minutes
- U18/U19: 2 x 45 minutes
The ECNL may adjust game lengths for any competition at any time. Halftime length is not stated in Rule 3.18.

## Substitutions (Rule 3.9)
### U13 and U14: substitution moments (Rule 3.9.2)
- Unlimited player substitutions within THREE "Substitution Moments" in each half, plus halftime.
- A Substitution Moment is a stoppage when the referee allows the coach to substitute.
- If both teams substitute in the same stoppage, it counts as one of the three moments for BOTH teams.
- Any eligible player may be substituted in each moment (re-entry is allowed).
- A head-injury evaluation substitution (Rule 3.9.4) does not count as a moment at U13/U14.

### U15 to U18/U19: maximum seven per half, no re-entry in the half (Rule 3.9.1)
- Maximum of SEVEN (7) substitutions in any half.
- A player substituted in a half may NOT re-enter for the rest of that half.
- Also applies to U15-U18 ECNL Conference League Playoffs and Finals.

### Head injury (temporary concussion substitution)
- A player suspected of a head injury may be temporarily substituted for evaluation without counting against the
  team's substitutions, and may re-enter if cleared (replacing the temporary substitute at the first opportunity).
- If not cleared, the temporary substitute becomes permanent.
- Heat and weather: substitution rules may be modified for safety (Rule 3.9.4 Heat and Weather Exceptions).

## Overtime
- Regular season games can end tied. In ECNL Playoffs/Finals, overtime procedures are designated by the ECNL (Rule 4.4).
