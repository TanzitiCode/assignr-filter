# NAL (National Academy League) quick reference: match length and substitutions

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16 from the official document(s) in this folder. If this sheet and the full document ever disagree, the full document wins.

Sources: NAL Technical Standards (2026-27 season page) and NAL 2025-2026 Referee Handout.

## Match length
- U13: 2 x 35-minute halves, 10-minute halftime
- U14 and U15: 2 x 40-minute halves, 15-minute halftime
- U16, U17, U19: 2 x 45-minute halves, 15-minute halftime

## Substitutions
- Unlimited substitutions over THREE moments per half, plus halftime.
- Re-entry is allowed.
- Head injury: a player being evaluated may be substituted without it counting as a substitution moment. If cleared,
  they may re-enter at any stoppage (replacing the original substitute), also not counted as a moment.

## Other
- Match day roster: 18 players. Players may play only 90 minutes per day across a maximum of 2 matches
  (goalkeepers are exempt from the 90-minute limit).
- Lightning: stop play; resume 30 minutes after the last thunder/lightning (the clock restarts with each new flash or thunder).
