# NCSL quick reference: half length and substitutions

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16 from the official document(s) in this folder. If this sheet and the full document ever disagree, the full document wins.

Source: NCSL Rules for Referees (ncsl-soccer.com), retrieved 2026-10-16. "All FIFA Laws of the Game apply except as modified."

## Half length by age group
- U9-U11: 30-minute halves (1 referee)
- U12: 35-minute halves (1 referee)
- U13-U14: 35-minute halves (3 referees)
- U15-U16: 40-minute halves (3 referees)
- U17-U19: 45-minute halves (3 referees)
- Halftime: 5 minutes for all ages

## Substitutions
- Allowed at the referee's discretion at ANY stoppage of play (not limited to goal kicks, kick-offs, or own throw-ins).
- Only limitation: an entering player may not enter to immediately take a penalty kick.
- Players may leave the field from wherever they choose unless instructed otherwise by the referee.
- Number of substitutions and re-entry are unlimited (no limit is stated).

## Other
- Grace period: 15 minutes; minimum 7 players (U11-U19) or 6 players (U9-U10) to start.
- Ball: size 4 for U9-U12, size 5 for U13-U19.
