# UPSL Academy quick reference: match length and substitutions

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16 from the official document(s) in this folder. If this sheet and the full document ever disagree, the full document wins.

Source: UPSL Academy Handbook 2024-25 (most recent found; check with your assignor for a newer edition).
All games follow the current FIFA Laws of the Game except as modified by UPSL Academy rules.

## Match length
- U13: 35-minute halves, 10-minute halftime
- U15: 40-minute halves, 10-minute halftime
- U17: 45-minute halves, 15-minute halftime

## Substitutions
- Unlimited substitutions and re-entry, BUT:
  - each team gets FIVE (5) substitution moments per GAME (halftime does not count as a moment);
  - a maximum of 3 players may be subbed in during one moment;
  - a player substituted in the first half may not re-enter until the second half;
  - a player substituted in the second half may re-enter only once.
- A substitution for evaluation of a head injury does not count toward the team's substitution moments.
- 3-person referee crew at each game.
