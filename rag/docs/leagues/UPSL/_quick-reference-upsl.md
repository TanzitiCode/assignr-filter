# UPSL quick reference: match length and substitutions

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16 from the official document(s) in this folder. If this sheet and the full document ever disagree, the full document wins.

Source: UPSL Spring 2023 Rules & Regulations Handbook (the most recent UPSL handbook found; check with your assignor
for a newer edition).

## Match length
- 90 minutes: two 45-minute halves plus referee's added time. Interval between halves: 15 minutes.
- Playoffs: if tied, two 15-minute periods of extra time, then kicks from the penalty mark.

## Substitutions
- Game day roster up to 20; only 18 can play (11 starters + 7 subs).
- Maximum of SEVEN (7) substitutions per game. NO re-entry.
- A player who re-enters after being substituted is an illegal player and the game results in a forfeit.
