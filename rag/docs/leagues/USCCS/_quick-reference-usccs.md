# USCCS (US College Club Soccer) quick reference: match length and substitutions

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16 from the official document(s) in this folder. If this sheet and the full document ever disagree, the full document wins.

Source: USCCS By-Laws / League Handbook (collegeclubsoccer.com), retrieved 2026-10-16.
Playing rules: the most recent IFAB Laws of the Game, except the exceptions in the handbook (NOT NCAA rules).

## Match length
- Two 45-minute halves (Rule 4.01).
- Regular season games may end in a draw. Playoff games may not; ties go to the tournament tie-breaker procedure.
- Weather: if 45 minutes have been completed, the result is final.

## Substitutions
- Unlimited substitution at ANY stoppage during the game (Rule 4.05).

## Discipline
- No suspensions for yellow card accumulation during a season (Rule 9.03).
- Any send-off: minimum one-game suspension, served in the next USCCS league game (Rule 9.04).
- Check-in: player must be on the printed roster and show a university photo ID (or digital).
