# VHSL (Virginia High School League) quick reference: NFHS rules vs IFAB

> Quick reference compiled 2026-10-16. VHSL plays under NFHS (National Federation of State High School Associations)
> soccer rules, NOT the IFAB Laws of the Game, with any VHSL modifications on top. The VHSL's own soccer
> modifications (regular-season overtime, tournament tie-breakers) were NOT available when this was compiled:
> check the current VHSL Handbook or your assignor's spring rules sheet (e.g. CSOA) and add it to this folder.

Source: NFHS "A Comparative Study of Rules and Laws, 2026-27 Soccer Guide" (NCAA vs NFHS vs IFAB),
nfhs-ncaa-ifab-comparative-study-2026-27.pdf in this folder.

## Game length (NFHS Rule 7)
- Two 40-minute periods (halves) or four 20-minute quarters.
- Halftime: 10 minutes, unless otherwise agreed by the coaches.
- (IFAB, for comparison: two 45-minute halves, halftime not to exceed 15 minutes.)

## Overtime (NFHS Rule 7)
- Overtime is allowed by the State Association, up to 20 minutes maximum.
- Post-season tie-breaking procedures are left to State Associations (for VHSL, see the VHSL Handbook).
- 5 minutes between the end of the game and the first OT period; 2 minutes between OT periods.

## Substitutions (NFHS Rule 3), very different from IFAB
- Unlimited substitutions. Re-entry: "Substituted players may re-enter the game an unlimited number of times." (IFAB: substituted players may not re-enter, unless local/youth competition rules allow it.)
- Either team may substitute an unlimited number FROM THE BENCH: between periods, after a goal, during an injury,
  or when the game is temporarily suspended.
- Either team may substitute an unlimited number IF THE SUBSTITUTE CHECKED IN BEFORE play was stopped: on a goal
  kick, during a caution (the cautioned player must leave; their substitute may come from the bench), during a
  disqualification, or when a player must leave because of equipment or disease concern.
- Throw-in: the team in possession may substitute; the other team may only substitute if the team in possession is
  also substituting. Substitutes must have checked in before play was stopped.
- All substitutes must be beckoned on by the referee.
- Goalkeeper sent off: the team plays short; the goalkeeper must be replaced by a field player, or by an incoming
  substitute (who can come from the bench), in which case a field player must also leave.
- (IFAB, for comparison: limited substitutions set by the competition, normally no re-entry.)
